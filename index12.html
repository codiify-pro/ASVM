Frequently Asked Questions

Is ASVM completely free to use?

Yes! ASVM is a 100% free platform. We don't require any sign-ups, subscriptions, or hidden fees. Just create a room and start watching.

What is the difference between Simple and Complex rooms?

In a Simple room, every user controls their own video player independently. In a Complex room, the player is synchronized; if the host pauses the video, it pauses for everyone.

Can I delete a room after I'm done?

Absolutely. You can delete any room directly from your "Recent Rooms" dashboard on the homepage, or from the sidebar menu inside the room itself.

Does the chat data save forever?

No. ASVM is designed for privacy. Once a room is deleted or becomes inactive, all chat logs and video data are permanently erased from our databases.
