Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

At ASVM, your privacy and security are our top priorities. We built this platform so you can watch videos and chat with your friends without worrying about trackers, logs, or invasive data collection. This policy explains exactly how we handle the minimal data we process.

100% Ephemeral Rooms & Chats

What happens in the room, stays in the room. Once your streaming session ends and the room becomes inactive, the room itself—along with every single chat message sent inside it—is securely and permanently deleted from our database. We do not keep chat logs, and we cannot recover deleted messages.

1. Information We Process

We believe in absolute minimalism when it comes to user data. In order to provide the watch party service, we process the following temporary information:

  • Display Names: When you join a room, you are asked for a name. This name is stored locally on your device (using Local Storage) so you don't have to type it every time. It is only shared within the active room so others know who is typing.
  • Media Links: The video URLs you paste are Base64-encoded before being temporarily held in our database to broadcast to your room. We do not monitor, track, or save your viewing history.
  • Real-time Chat: Chat messages are processed in real-time to broadcast to other users in your room.

2. Data Deletion Protocol

ASVM operates on a strict "Auto-Wipe" system to guarantee your privacy. After a period of inactivity or when a room is manually terminated, our infrastructure automatically purges the Room ID, the encoded video link, and the entire chat history. Because this data is ephemeral, it cannot be handed over to third parties or retrieved later.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We solely use browser Local Storage and functional cookies to enhance your experience. Specifically, we use them to:

  • Remember the Display Name you entered.
  • Remember the ID of the last room you visited, allowing you to easily rejoin if you accidentally close your tab.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Third-Party Services

When you stream a video using a YouTube link, the native YouTube player is embedded into our site. In these instances, you are subject to YouTube's standard privacy policies regarding video playback. However, your private room chats remain securely contained within ASVM infrastructure and are completely inaccessible to third-party media providers.
